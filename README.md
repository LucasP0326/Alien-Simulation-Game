# Alien Observation Station: Earth

A small browser game inspired by Reigns, where you play as an alien observer directing major events on Earth through a series of branching decisions.

## How to run

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Make choices as the alien station reacts to crises, diplomacy, climate events, and the future of humanity.
3. Try to shape Earth toward a hopeful future without losing control of the station or overwhelming the human population with fear.

## Gameplay notes

- Most events are two-choice decisions.
- Some events are chained sequences that unlock follow-up decisions.
- Your actions alter metrics such as empathy, influence, fear, hope, control, and station integrity.
- Different ending states are triggered by the values you leave Earth with.

## Files

- `index.html` — structure and UI
- `style.css` — visual design
- `script.js` — game logic and branching story
